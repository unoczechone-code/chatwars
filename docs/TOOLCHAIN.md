## Renderer

**Compatibility (`gl_compatibility`)**, chosen for the first spike.

Basis: the Godot 4.7.2 docs state the iOS Simulator supports Compatibility only. That is *not* proof of behaviour on a physical iPhone — that remains `UNVERIFIED` until measured on the device.

Do not switch renderer after the baseline is built without a rebuild and a fresh device test.
